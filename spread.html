Small screen. Full formulas.

Tap a cell, edit above, then tap ✓ or Return. Double-tap a cell to start typing. Enter advances one row. Four columns fit on a narrow screen; ‹ and › reveal more.

Use text, numbers, or formulas:
=A1+B1*2
=SUM(A1:A10)
=AVERAGE(A1:B5)
=MIN(A1:A5)
=MAX(A1:A5)
=COUNT(A1:A5)
=ROUND(A1/3,2)
=SQRT(A1)

Arithmetic supports + − * / ^ and parentheses. There are 26 columns and 100 rows. Circular references show #CYCLE!; invalid formulas show #ERROR!.

Changes are saved in this browser when available. Save downloads a backup with formulas; Open restores one. CSV exports calculated values. Keep a backup before clearing browser data.
